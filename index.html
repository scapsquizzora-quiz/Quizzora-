KERALA'S PREMIER INTER-SCHOOL QUIZ CHAMPIONSHIP

ABOUT QUIZZORA 2026

QUIZZORA is the prestigious annual inter-school state-level quiz championship organized by the School of Commerce and Professional Studies (SCAPS) at Marian College Kuttikkanam (Autonomous). Designed to celebrate intellectual excellence, rapid recall, and collaborative brilliance, Quizzora offers school teams across Kerala a world-class platform to test their knowledge, represent their institutions, and compete for state-wide championship honors.

State-Level Stage

Brings together ambitious young minds from schools across all Kerala districts to compete in a healthy, electrifying intellectual arena.

9 Regional Prelim Centres

Convenient examination hubs across Kottayam, Idukki, Pathanamthitta, and Ernakulam, ensuring every school can easily participate.

Grand Finale at Marian

Top qualifying teams advance to the live buzzer and audio-visual stage rounds on 19 November 2026 at Marian College Kuttikkanam.

₹25,000+ Prizes & Trophy

Grand cash awards, the Quizzora State Rolling Trophy, gold and silver medals, and official certificates for all participants.

ELIGIBILITY, CATEGORIES & FORMAT

COMPETITION DETAILS

Quizzora is structured into two dedicated school categories, ensuring grade-appropriate competition and specialized focus for commerce aspirants.

CATEGORY 1

JUNIOR QUIZ

Students Studying in Classes 8, 9 & 10
General Knowledge Quiz designed to test sharp mental agility, current affairs, Indian heritage, scientific curiosity, and regional knowledge.
General Knowledge Indian History Geography Science & Technology Sports Current Affairs Logical Reasoning Famous Personalities Kerala General Knowledge
CATEGORY 2

SENIOR COMMERCE QUIZ

Students Studying in Classes 11 & 12
State-Level Commerce Quiz focusing on core enterprise principles, financial awareness, macroeconomic policies, and global business acumen.
Business Studies Accountancy Economics Banking & Finance Entrepreneurship Stock Market Basics Marketing Business Personalities Indian Economy Financial Awareness

TOURNAMENT STRUCTURE & RULES

Team Structure

Teams must consist of two students from the same school. Schools may register multiple teams across both categories.

Preliminary Stage

Conducted across 9 official centres (04–14 Nov 2026) and on-campus (19 Nov 2026). Written MCQ format testing speed and accuracy.

Semi-Finals & Grand Finale

Top qualifying school teams from prelims advance to the live buzzer and visual rounds at Marian College Kuttikkanam on 19 Nov 2026.

Prizes & Recognition

Over ₹25,000 cash prizes, State Champions Rolling Trophy, medals for winners, and official certificates for all participants.

HOW TO REGISTER FOR QUIZZORA

Follow these 4 easy steps to register your school team for the Quizzora State-Level Competition.

01

Form Your School Team
